TITLE:
Listen Alone

Characters:
Nora: 24 years old, dark green jacket, dark jeans, long black hair tied back, carrying a small backpack and an old camera.
Elias: Nora's older brother, 31 years old, faded blue jacket, short dark hair, seen only in old photographs and recordings.
Unknown Man: Tall adult male, dark waterproof coat, face rarely visible.

Location:
An abandoned mountain weather station built on a cliff above a remote coastal valley. Fog, rain, old concrete buildings, rusted antennas, narrow corridors, underground rooms.

Props:
Old camera, cassette recorder, flashlight, metal key, photographs, dusty maps, damaged computer, flashlight, old generator, SD card.

Style:
Realistic cinematic mystery thriller, natural environments, grounded realism, detailed textures, atmospheric fog, realistic human movement, subtle suspense, dark neutral lighting, no supernatural elements.

Moment:
Nora drives alone along a narrow mountain road toward an abandoned weather station.

Vibe:
Isolated, uneasy, mysterious.

Style:
Overcast daylight, realistic cinematic photography, cold atmosphere.

Script:
Nora had not returned to the mountain in eight years.
The road looked exactly as she remembered it.
Broken asphalt.
Dead grass.
Fog covering the valley below.
At the top of the hill stood the old weather station where her brother Elias had disappeared without a trace.
She had spent years believing he was dead.
Today, someone had sent her a photograph proving otherwise.

The photograph showed the weather station entrance.
It had been taken yesterday.
In the corner of the image was something Nora immediately recognized.
Her brother's old red backpack.
The same backpack he had been carrying the night he vanished.
There was no message with the photograph.
Only coordinates.
Nora looked toward the building.
For the first time, she noticed that one of the windows was slightly open.
